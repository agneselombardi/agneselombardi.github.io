In the film *Her*, Theodore — a professional writer of love letters — begins a romantic relationship with Samantha, the operating system on his computer, characterized by her charming and seductive voice.

This is, of course, science fiction. But how far are we really from this reality? And where did it all begin?

This is the first lesson of *Introduction to Computational Linguistics*. To understand where we are today, we first need to understand what this field is and how it has evolved.

This course is designed for beginners and non-linguists alike.
